Hydroclimatic Extremes & associated risk

Early-career researcher in Earth observation and geospatial data science, working on hydroclimatic hazards and how a changing climate shapes extreme events and the risks they create for people, cities and infrastructure.My research began with satellite-based mapping of land and urban change and has grown into a broader question: how do extreme rainfall, drought and heat, and the processes behind them such as antecedent soil moisture, turn into real-world risks?I am particularly interested in how these extremes and their associated risks vary across space and time, which hydrologic conditions build up to events such as slope failure, and how urban growth and land-use change influence them.To explore these questions, I combine Earth observation (optical and SAR), weather and climate data, geospatial analysis and explainable machine learning.Ultimately, I hope to contribute research that links process understanding with better hazard prediction, climate resilience and informed decision-making.
