Tejas Deshmukh

urban Hydroclimatic Extremes & associated risk

Early- career interdisciplinary researcher with a background in Earth observation and Geospatial data Sciences, specializing in Hydroclimatic and atmospheric remote sensing exploring how urbanisation and a changing climate shape extreme events and the risks they create for people and cities.
My research journey began with satellite based mapping of land and urban growth and has gradually expanded toward a broader question: how do extreme rainfall, heat, and air pollution translate into real-world risks? I am particularly interested in understanding how these hazards vary across space and time, how urban development influences them, and why their impacts differ across communities.
To explore these questions, I bring together Earth observation, weather and climate data, geospatial analysis, and predictive AI/ML modelling, with the aim of improving our understanding of urban climate extremes and their associated health, social, and economic risks.
Ultimately, I hope to contribute to research that connects scientific understanding with better risk assessment, climate resilience, and informed decision-making.
