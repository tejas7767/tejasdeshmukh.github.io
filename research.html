Research

Understanding a changing Earth.

My research focuses on intersection of urban hydroclimatic extremes (heat, rainfall and air pollution) and the climate-health risks they create.
I combine Earth observation, weather and climate model output, and machine learning to understand how extremes develop, how urban change shapes their impacts, and what this means for risk assessment.

Geospatial and Multivariate Analysis of the Relationship Between Urban Air Pollutants and Heat Stress Across Indian Metropolitan

IIT Madras

Developed a Python-based workflow for 550+ CPCB stations (180+ cities) with 5-year hourly data covering 11 air pollutants and five meteorological variables: ambient air temperature (AT), relative humidity (RH), wind speed (WS), wind direction (WD), and solar radiation (SR).

Spatial-temporal variation of urban flood susceptibility under urban expansion and changing hydro-climatic conditions, using Sentinel-1 SAR-derived flood inventories, machine learning, SHAP explainability and climate projections.
