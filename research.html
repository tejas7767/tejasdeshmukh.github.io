Research

Understanding a changing Earth.

My research focuses on hydroclimatic and atmospheric extremes (heat, rainfall and air pollution) and the risks they create for people, cities and infrastructure.I combine Earth observation, weather and climate model output, and machine learning to understand how these extremes develop, how land and urban change shape their impacts, and what this means for hazard and risk assessment.

Explainable Machine Learning for Landslide Susceptibility, Devikulam Taluk (Idukki, Western Ghats, india)

IIT Madras • Nov 2025-June 2026
Supervisor: Dr. Subbarao Pichuka, IIT Madras

Rainfall-triggered landslides are a recurring hazard in the Western Ghats of Kerala. This study maps landslide susceptibility across Devikulam Taluk (about 1,204 km², elevation up to 2,685 m). It compares four machine-learning models and then uses SHAP explainable AI to show why the best model makes its predictions. The aim was a map that is both accurate and understandable, so it can support hazard assessment and planning.

PM2.5 estimation from CPCB observations , Maharashtra

Independent project · Nov 2025


• Data: CPCB ground-based PM₂.₅ observations, fine-mode
   aerosol optical depth (FAOD) derived from satellite data, and
   meteorological variables.
•  Method: Compared Random Forest (RF), Multiple Linear
   Regression (MLR), Linear Regression (LR), and Lasso Regression     for PM₂.₅ estimation.
• Outcome: Developed a spatial PM₂.₅ estimation framework for   
  Maharashtra and mapped spatial/temporal variability, identifying
   higher-concentration areas around Mumbai, Thane and Pune. RF
   performed best, with R² = 0.87, RMSE = 12.57 µg/m³, and MAE = 
   6.96 µg/m³.

Groundwater potential and trend assessment, Karnataka

 Independent project · Dec 2025

• Processed and analysed 18,157 groundwater observations from 217 CGWB monitoring wells spanning 22 districts (2000–2022). 
• Developed statewide and seasonal groundwater potential models using terrain, climate, LULC, NDVI, NDWI, lithology, geomorphology.
• 88 wells with declining groundwater levels, 20 wells with improving groundwater conditions, and 109 remained statistically stable.
• Depletion : In Belgaum (17 wells) and Kalaburagi (16 wells), whereas increasing trends : In high-rainfall districts such as Dakshin Kannada, Kodagu, and Udupi
• Generated groundwater potential zones, groundwater trend
 maps and identified recharge/depletion hotspots using Mann-Kendall, Sen's Slope, ML algorithms 
 Skills: Geostatistics, Python, ArcGIS Pro, DEM Analysis, LULC Analysis, Spatial Analysis

Dynamic Forest Fire Susceptibility Assessment under Future Climate Change Using CMIP6 Projections and Multi-Sensor Remote Sensing: A Case Study of Uttarakhand, India 

 Independent project · May 2026

• Analysed forest-fire dynamics in Uttarakhand using MODIS/VIIRS fire observations, Sentinel-2/Landsat vegetation indices, LST, soil moisture, precipitation and terrain variables. 
• Developed an explainable machine-learning(XAI) framework to identify climatic, vegetation and topographic controls on forest-fire susceptibility and generate spatial fire-risk maps.
• Integrated CMIP6 SSP245 and SSP585 projections to assess future changes in temperature, precipitation and drought-related fire conditions and identify emerging climate-sensitive forest-fire zones
