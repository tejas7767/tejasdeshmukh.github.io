Urban flood susceptibility and exposure under climate and urban-growth scenarios, Chennai

IIT Madras 

Spatial-temporal variation of urban flood susceptibility under urban expansion and changing hydro-climatic conditions, using Sentinel-1 SAR-derived flood inventories, machine learning, SHAP explainability and climate projections.

Workflow

A workflow linking satellite flood mapping, machine learning and future urban growth to identify where urban flood risk is highest under climate extremes. Manuscript under review.
1. Flood inventory: map past flood extent from Sentinel-1 SAR to build the training inventory.
2. Conditioning factors: assemble rainfall, terrain, future land use and imperviousness, distance to water bodies and drainage network.
3. Susceptibility modelling: train Random Forest, XGBoost and ANN models, and use SHAP to show which factors drive flood susceptibility.
4. Future scenarios: combine CMIP6 climate projections with simulated future urban growth to assess how susceptibility changes.
5. Ward-level exposure: combine susceptibility with exposure to identify the most flood-vulnerable wards.
