Land cover dynamics and surface energy balance, Guwahati

Independent project · Feb 2025


•  Conducted multi-temporal satellite image classification to map
  LULC changes in Guwahati.
•  Derived and analyzed Land Surface Temperature (LST) and
  albedo from thermal and optical bands (using ArcGIS Pro/Google
   Earth Engine).
•  Also Integrated vegetation (NDVI), built-up (NDBI), water
    indices (NDWI), and terrain factors to study urbanization and
    environmental change.
